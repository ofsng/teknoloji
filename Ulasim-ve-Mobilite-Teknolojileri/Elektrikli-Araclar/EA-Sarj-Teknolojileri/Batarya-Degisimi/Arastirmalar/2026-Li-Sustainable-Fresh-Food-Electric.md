# Sustainable Fresh-Food Electric Vehicle Routing with Load-Dependent Energy Consumption and Battery Swapping: A PSO-Guided Adaptive Multi-Operator Genetic Algorithm

## 1. Araştırmanın özeti

> Fresh-food distribution with electric vehicles requires coordinated routing and fleet decisions under perishability, time window, and battery constraints. Simplified energy consumption models and limited consideration of fleet heterogeneity can overlook the effects of changing payloads on these decisions. This study formulates an optimization model integrating load-dependent electricity consumption, heterogeneous vehicle capacities, battery swapping, product spoilage, and soft time windows. A particle swarm optimization-guided adaptive multi-operator genetic algorithm (PSO-AMOGA) is developed, with an outer PSO procedure optimizing the selection probabilities of crossover and mutation operators. Numerical experiments show that PSO-guided operator selection improves solution quality and yields lower total costs than fixed-probability operator selection, while fleet-composition decisions require balancing vehicle-deployment costs, energy consumption, and battery-swapping requirements. Sensitivity analyses further highlight the importance of jointly optimizing vehicle assignment, routing, and battery-swapping decisions according to demand characteristics, product perishability, and delivery requirements. These findings support sustainable fresh-food distribution by informing the joint management of energy consumption, product spoilage, and operating costs through coordinated vehicle assignment, routing, and battery-swapping decisions.
