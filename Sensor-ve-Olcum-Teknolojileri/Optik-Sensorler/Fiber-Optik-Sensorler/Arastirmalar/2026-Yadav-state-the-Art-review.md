# A state-of-the-Art review on side-polished fiber optic sensors: fabrication, principles, sensing performance, and applications

## 1. Araştırmanın özeti

> Side-Polished Fiber (SPF) sensors have emerged as evolving class of fiber optic sensors that leverage the enhanced interaction between the evanescent field of the optical signal and the surrounding medium by polishing away the cladding until it reaches remarkably close to the fiber core. This paper reviews the SPF-based sensors in detail. First, the physical characteristics and fabrication process of SPF has been discussed. The structural and geometrical properties of the SPF strongly determine the light propagation and its interaction with the external medium. Fabrication remains one of the major challenges in the design of SPF sensors. Mechanical and laser-based fabrication methods are widely used in development of SPF sensors. The following section discusses the operating principle and underlying sensing mechanism of SPF. This engineered light-matter interaction translates minute refractive index (RI) changes, surface binding events, or other environmental characteristics into optical signals, thereby enabling robust label-free detection across biomedical, chemical, and physical sensing domains. Subsequently, a comprehensive classification of SPF configuration has been presented, and their respective characteristics have been discussed. Finally, the potential end use applications of SPF sensors have been comprehensively reviewed.
