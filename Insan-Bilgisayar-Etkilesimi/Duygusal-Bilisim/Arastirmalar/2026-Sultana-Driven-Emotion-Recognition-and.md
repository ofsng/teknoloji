# AI-Driven Emotion Recognition and Decision-Making in Mental Health: Ethical and Psychological Implications for Early Detection and Human-Computer Interaction

## 1. Araştırmanın özeti

> Artificial intelligence (AI) is being integrated across various fields. AI advancements have catalyzed a paradigm shift in psychological assessment and intervention, aiming to rapidly transform how mental health and psychological imbalance are assessed, monitored, and treated. In this research, we examine the role of AI in the early detection of affective and cognitive disorders, with a focus on depression, anxiety, and stress-related psychopathologies using emotion recognition technologies and predictive modeling. We explore how affective computing techniques such as facial expression analysis, speech recognition, and natural language processing are integrated within digital mental health platforms for efficient therapeutic alliance, accurate clinical decision-making, and boosted user engagement. In addition, this research investigates the effects of human-computer interaction in an AI-powered mental health context, addressing constructs such as user trust, engagement, and cognitive reliance on autonomous systems. A critical analysis of ethical and governance factors demonstrated several pitfalls that hinder AI-driven mental health adoption. These include algorithm opacity and bias, non-compliance with regulatory frameworks and data protection policies, and inequitable access to existing platforms. The interdisciplinary synthesis employed in this study provides evidence-based insight with strategic recommendations for developing, deploying, and regulating AI-driven mental health solutions in healthcare systems.
