# Comparing the refreshing and single batch powder reuse methods for AlSi7Mg produced by laser powder bed fusion

## 1. Araştırmanın özeti

> Laser powder bed fusion is used primarily to produce metallic components and allows for a high degree of geometric freedom and low material usage, although this is reliant on powder reuse, which is now common practice in industrial and academic settings. However, the methodological details of powder reuse are not regularly considered and so current irregularities in practice have the potential to result in degraded and inconsistent properties for parts manufactured with reused powders. Therefore, this work focuses on a comparison between two powder reuse methods to produce AlSi7Mg parts by laser powder bed fusion: powder refreshing and single batch powder reuse, finding that the powder reuse method influences powder characteristics to a greater extent than mechanical properties. Powder flowability improved for the refreshing method, seeing a 20.3% reduction in Hall flow time over the first four manufacturing runs, while the single batch method changed from non-flowing to flowing powder over the same period. Powder magnesium content also saw a larger decrease during the single batch method of 9.4% compared to 7.1% for the refreshing method. No significant changes to tensile properties were seen for the refreshing method, alongside a minor reduction in high cycle fatigue resistance at a stress amplitude of 200 MPa, whereas no changes in either tensile or fatigue properties were observed for the single batch method. These results show that while powder characteristics are dependent on the powder reuse method changes to the mechanical properties of parts are minimal, indicating both reuse methods are viable powder management strategies. However, different powder batches were used for each powder reuse method which may have influenced the degree to which these changes can be attributed solely to each specific powder reuse method.
