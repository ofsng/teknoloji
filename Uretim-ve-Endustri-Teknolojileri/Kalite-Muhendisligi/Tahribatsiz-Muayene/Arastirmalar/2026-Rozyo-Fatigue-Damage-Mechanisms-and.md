# Fatigue Damage Mechanisms and Complementary Non-Destructive Testing of Thin-Walled Structures with Various Composite Material Lay-Ups

## 1. Araştırmanın özeti

> This paper focuses on the fatigue response of composite structures, considering complementary (different) non-destructive testing (NDT) techniques. The research concerns thin-walled profiles with a notch and several different composite material layer arrangements (composite lay-ups). The research determined the influence of the layer arrangement on fatigue strength and laminate damage forms. A fatigue testing device, passive thermography to assess thermal changes, acoustic emission to detect damage initiation and digital microscopy to analyse damage forms were utilised in the research. The correct parameters for fatigue testing were determined during the test trials to ensure that the test specimens did not lose their load-carrying capacity. The research aimed to analyse the phenomenon of composite failure during fatigue testing in quantitative and qualitative aspects. The study only considered fatigue response in the context of permissible operating loads-excluding tests that took into account the total failure of the specimens.
