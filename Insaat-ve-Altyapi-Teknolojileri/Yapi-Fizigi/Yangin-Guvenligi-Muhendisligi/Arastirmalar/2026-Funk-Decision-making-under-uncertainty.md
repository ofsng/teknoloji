# Decision making under uncertainty in fire safety engineering: An industrial Battery Energy Storage System Round Robin Study

## 1. Araştırmanın özeti

> Fire safety engineering relies on engineering judgement when empirical evidence is insufficient. This challenge is particularly evident for Battery Energy Storage Systems, where limited large-scale fire data require engineers to make assumptions under epistemic uncertainty. Despite the central role of engineering judgement in design fire selection, little empirical evidence exists on how experienced engineers develop representative design fires. This study examines this process through an international Round Robin in which experienced fire safety professionals independently addressed the same BESS consultancy task using identical project information. The submissions were analyzed to compare how participants identified scenario clusters, selected and represented design fires, and justified their engineering assumptions. The results show that the variability was evident in how participants interpreted the engineering task and translated incomplete knowledge into engineering assumptions. Four recurring reasoning strategies were identified: construction, transfer, analogy and constraint. These findings highlight the importance of transparent and well-justified engineering assumptions in fire safety engineering.
