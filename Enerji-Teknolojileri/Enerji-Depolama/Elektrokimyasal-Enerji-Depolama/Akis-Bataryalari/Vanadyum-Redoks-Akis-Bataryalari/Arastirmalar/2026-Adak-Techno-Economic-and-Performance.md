# Techno-Economic and Performance Assessment of Battery versus Green Hydrogen Storage Systems for Large-Scale Grid Decarbonization

## 1. Araştırmanın özeti

> With an energy evolution taking hold, net-zero ambitions rapidly spread from the household to the organization to the grid.This paper conducts a systematic techno-economic and performance comparison of battery energy storage systems (BESS, specifically Lithium Iron Phosphate [〖"LiFePO" 〗_4 / LFP] and Vanadium Redox Flow Batteries [VRFB]) versus green hydrogen energy storage systems (GHESS, utilizing polymer electrolyte membrane [PEM] electrolyzers, geological cavern/compressed storage, and PEM/combined cycle fuel conversion) across discharge durations of 4 to 120 hours.A techno-economic assessment evaluates Levelized Cost of Storage (LCOS), Round-Trip Efficiency (RTE), footprint, cyclic degradation, and capital scaling metrics.The study found that while BESS achieves higher RTE (72.0% to 84.0%) and clear cost leadership below 8 hours, its capital cost barrier at higher durations scales steeply due to linear cell-stack addition.In contrast, the GHESS architecture decouples power capacity from energy capacity, achieving marginal capacity costs below $3/"kWh" (in geological salt caverns) and establishing economic crossover dominance from 14 to 16 hours of continuous discharge onward.A multi-objective hybrid architecture is modeled, demonstrating that pairing BESS for intra-day balancing with GHESS for synoptic, multi-day reserves minimizes total system curtailment and yields an optimized levelized cost profile for zero-emission bulk power utilities.
