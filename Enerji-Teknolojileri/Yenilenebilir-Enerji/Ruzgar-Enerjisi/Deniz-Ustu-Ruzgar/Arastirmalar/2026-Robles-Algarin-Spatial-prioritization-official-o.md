# Spatial prioritization of official offshore wind zones in the Colombian Caribbean: A comparative assessment of fixed-bottom and floating areas

## 1. Araştırmanın özeti

> This study spatially prioritizes the official offshore wind zones defined for the Colombian Caribbean, distinguishing fixed-bottom (FX) and floating (FL) areas. Rather than delineating new marine areas, the analysis complements Colombia's offshore wind roadmap by comparing its existing polygons. Sixteen variables representing wind resource, physical-marine conditions, infrastructure and logistics, environmental and biodiversity constraints, and marine uses were standardized by fuzzy logic on a common 1–5 suitability scale. Criterion weights were derived with the Best-Worst Method from a panel of 13 experts and integrated through weighted map algebra. Mean suitability within each official polygon was used for separate intragroup rankings. FX-6 and FX-5 ranked highest among fixed-bottom areas, whereas FL-5, FL-6, and FL-2 led the floating group. Sensitivity tests based on equal weights, criterion perturbation and exclusion, dimension-level scenarios, and 2000 Monte Carlo simulations confirmed a stable upper tier, particularly for FX-6 and the FL-5/FL-6/FL-2 group. The resulting prioritization constitutes an early-stage analytical input for targeting prefeasibility studies, grid-connection assessment, port preparation, and marine environmental evaluation while preserving the official roadmap polygons as the units of analysis.
