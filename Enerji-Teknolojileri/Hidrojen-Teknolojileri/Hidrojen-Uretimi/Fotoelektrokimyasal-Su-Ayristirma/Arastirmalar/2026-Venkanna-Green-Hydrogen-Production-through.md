# Green Hydrogen Production through Photoelectrochemical Water Splitting

## 1. Araştırmanın özeti

> Photoelectrochemical (PEC) water splitting is a solar-fuel technology in which semiconductor photoelectrodes absorb sunlight, generate electronhole pairs, and drive the hydrogen evolution reaction (HER) and oxygen evolution reaction (OER) at electrochemical interfaces.The overall reaction is thermodynamically uphill and requires at least 1.23 V under standard conditions, while practical devices require additional energy to overcome kinetic overpotentials, transport losses, and recombination.This review summarizes the operating principles of PEC cells, semiconductor band alignment, interfacial charge transfer, reaction mechanisms, performance metrics, material classes, and engineering strategies.A comparative assessment of PEC water splitting, particulate photocatalysis, photovoltaic-powered electrolysis, and conventional steam methane reforming is presented.Although PEC systems offer direct solar-tochemical conversion and potentially compact integration, their commercialization is constrained by photoelectrode stability, scalable fabrication, gas separation, and long-term solar-to-hydrogen efficiency.
