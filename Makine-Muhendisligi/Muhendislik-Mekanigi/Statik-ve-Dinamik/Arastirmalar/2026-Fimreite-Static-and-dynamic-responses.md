# Static and dynamic responses of elastoplastic beams with rectangular and asymmetric I-profile cross section subjected to lateral pressure loads

## 1. Araştırmanın özeti

> This paper presents a simplified analytical method for predicting the static and dynamic responses of elastoplastic beams subjected to lateral pressure loads with arbitrary spatial distribution and temporal history. The method addresses beams with rectangular cross section as well as vertically asymmetric I-profiles for offshore applications. The Lagrange’s equation of motion is used to formulate the beam dynamic responses, while static force displacement curves are obtained by eliminating the kinetic energy terms. Generalized yield functions based on stress resultants are adopted to model interactions of internal reaction forces for each cross section. The deformation process is divided into four distinct stages, describing the evolution of internal forces considering the elastoplastic behavior, followed by a fifth stage representing elastic unloading after maximum deformation. The proposed method is validated through comparisons with nonlinear finite element analysis (NLFEA) conducted in ABAQUS, showing good agreement across elastic, elastoplastic, and fully plastic deformation regimes. This approach provides a fast and reliable tool for estimating structural responses to static and dynamic loads arising from wave impacts, explosions, and ice collisions.
