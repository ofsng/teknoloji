# Simultaneous Ammonia Nitrogen Removal and COD Reduction from Flue-Gas-Desulfurization Wastewater Using KH-550-Modified Tourmaline: Performance and Mechanisms

## 1. Araştırmanın özeti

> Flue-gas-desulfurization wastewater (FGDW) is difficult to treat because of its high salinity, weak acidity, and elevated NH3-N and COD concentrations. In this study, tourmaline (TM) was modified with the silane coupling agent KH-550 to produce TMKH550 for the simultaneous removal of NH3-N and reduction of COD from actual FGDW. Characterization confirmed successful grafting of –NH2 and Si–OH groups and formation of a Si–O–TM network, with an increase in mean pore diameter and a modest increase in specific surface area. Under the combined optimized conditions, the apparent removals were 60.2% for NH3-N and 17.5% for COD, corresponding to approximately 7.8- and 3.5-fold enhancements relative to pristine TM. A possible removal mechanism is proposed, involving electrostatic attraction and hydrogen bonding with NH4+, physical entrapment in pores, and a possible interfacial oxidative pathway associated with the spontaneous polarization of TM. This study provides a surface-engineered mineral material with promising potential for the advanced treatment of high-salinity refractory industrial wastewater.
