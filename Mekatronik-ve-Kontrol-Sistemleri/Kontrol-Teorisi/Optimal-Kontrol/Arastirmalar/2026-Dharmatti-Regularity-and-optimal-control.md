# Regularity and optimal control of nonlocal Cahn–Hilliard–Brinkman system with singular potential

## 1. Araştırmanın özeti

> The dynamics of two-phase incompressible, immiscible, isothermal fluids in porous media are described by the coupled Cahn–Hilliard–Brinkman (CHB) system, which combines a phase-field approach with a generalized Darcy–Brinkman momentum balance proposed by H. C. Brinkman (1949). In this work, we address the optimal control problem for a two-dimensional nonlocal CHB system with a logarithmic-type potential and degenerate mobility. The existence and regularity results are obtained by approximation methods. Further, we establish the uniqueness of the solution by deriving continuous dependence estimates. By treating the external forcing term in the velocity equation as a control variable, we prove the existence of an optimal control which minimizes a tracking-type cost functional. The differentiability properties of the control-to-state operator are studied to establish the first-order necessary optimality conditions. Moreover, the optimal control is characterized in terms of the adjoint variable. Furthermore, we studied existence results for the system associated with a non-smooth double-obstacle potential.
