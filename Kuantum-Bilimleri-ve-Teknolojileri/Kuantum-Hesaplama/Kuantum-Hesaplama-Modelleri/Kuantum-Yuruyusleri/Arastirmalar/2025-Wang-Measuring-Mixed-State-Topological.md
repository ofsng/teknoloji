# Measuring Mixed-State Topological Invariant in Open Photonic Quantum Walk

## 1. Araştırmanın özeti

> Pure-state manifestations of geometric phase are well established and have found applications across essentially all branches of physics, yet their generalization to mixed-state regimes remains largely unexplored experimentally. The Uhlmann geometric phase offers a natural extension of pure-state paradigms and can exhibit a topological character. However, observation of this invariant is impeded by the incompatibility between Uhlmann parallel transport and Hamiltonian dynamics, as well as the difficulty of preparing topologically nontrivial mixed states. To address this challenge, we report an experimentally accessible protocol for directly measuring the mixed-state topological invariant. By engineering controlled nonunitary dynamics in a photonic quantum walk, we prepare topologically nontrivial mixed states from a trivial initial state. Then, by machine learning the full density matrix in momentum space, we directly extract the quantized geometric phase of the nontrivial mixed states. Furthermore, we observe that this transition of the quantized mixed-state topological invariant from a trivial to a nontrivial value is closely associated with the emergence of a topological kink structure. These results highlight a geometric phase framework that naturally extends to open quantum systems both in and out of thermal equilibrium.
