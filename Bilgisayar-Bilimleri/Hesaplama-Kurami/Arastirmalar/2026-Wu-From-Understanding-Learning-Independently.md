# From “Understanding” to “Learning Independently”: Cultivating Autonomous Learning Skills in Theory of Computation Course

## 1. Araştırmanın özeti

> Theory of Computation is a foundational course that requires students to understand formal mathematical definitions and apply logical reasoning independently. However, understanding a teacher’s explanation does not necessarily mean that students can interpret mathematical expressions or solve problems without assistance. This paper examines the cultivation of autonomous learning skills in a Theory of Computation course, focusing on the relationship between knowledge understanding and independent learning. The paper discusses the importance of distinguishing between understanding knowledge through instruction and developing the ability to understand knowledge independently. The paper then presents a layered approach to interpreting mathematical definitions, connecting formal expressions with written explanations and accessible oral interpretations. The approach aims to reduce initial barriers to understanding while helping students gradually return to formal definitions and interpret them independently. The paper argues that effective instruction should not only make knowledge understandable but also help students develop the capacity to understand and apply knowledge independently.
