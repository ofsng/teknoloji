# ULB: An ultra-lightweight block cipher

## 1. Araştırmanın özeti

> Lightweight block ciphers are essential for resource-constrained devices, requiring a careful balance between security and implementation efficiency. In this work, we propose a new lightweight block cipher ULB which has a smaller ASIC area than the ISO/IEC standard cipher SKINNY while providing comparable bounds under the evaluated differential, linear, and related-tweakey models. ULB operates on a 64-bit state with tweakey sizes of 64, 128, or 192 bits. Depending on the tweakey length, the cipher consists of 36, 40, or 44 rounds, respectively, following a compact SPN structure. For confusion layer, ULB employs a 4-bit S-box designed through a SAT-based search model, which achieves an area of 11 GE and a circuit depth of 3 under the UMC 180nm library, outperforming those used in existing lightweight ciphers. For the diffusion layer, we select a cost-efficient 4 × 4 binary matrix with a low XOR count and circuit depth. Experimental results show that ULB achieves a smaller ASIC area compared to SKINNY, with reductions of 15.77%, 12.48%, and 9.73% for the three corresponding variants. These results demonstrate the effectiveness of our component-level optimization approach, enabling area-efficient ultra-lightweight cipher constructions.
