# A Method for Identifying Insiders in Corporate Computer Networks

## 1. Araştırmanın özeti

> Relevance. Countering insider threats in corporate computer networks is one of the most complex problems in the field of information security. This is due to the fact that an insider is already located within the organization and has access to some of its information resources. They can also collect information on a frequent basis, disguising their activities as job responsibilities, thus becoming virtually indistinguishable from other employees. The purpose of this study is to develop a method for identifying organizational insiders based on a large number of indicators, the analytical interrelationships of which were previously obtained by the author. To achieve this goal, the necessary prerequisites were developed, expanding the author's model (previously published) and specialized for the current task, which made it possible to synthesize an appropriate framework for identifying insiders in computer networks. The underlying model of insider indicators formalizes the sequential receipt of user activity indicators on the network, produced by relevant information sources, from which aggregators calculate insider indicators in a standard manner, and then, using a specialized method, insider indicators. Based on the latter values, the model makes a decision regarding the presence of an insider in the corporate computer network. The method's input data is information about the corporate computer network and its users' actions, its parameters are expert settings, and its output is a decision on the presence of an insider, along with its rationale and credibility assessment. Hypothetical examples of each step's operation substantiate the method's overall effectiveness. Methods. analysis of scientific papers, basic analytical modeling, structural synthesis, hypothetical experiments. Results. A method for identifying insiders consisting of 30 steps, each of which is a separate block and is presented in a corresponding graphical diagram. In addition to standard steps, the method includes the following, specialized for the current task: accumulation and generalization of information by sources to generate indicators, use of a sliding window for them, partial calculation of aggregators with an incomplete set of data from input indicators, assessment of the model's solution reliability and its expert verification, structural and parametric adaptation of the model in the event of erroneous decisions, and direct implementation of countermeasures against insiders. The novelty lies both in the use of the author's model as the computational core of the method, and in the significant expansion of actions for identifying insiders (compared to those used), including a comprehensive representation of user characteristics and consideration of the validity of the decision. The practical significance of the method is determined by the level of detail that is suitable for its immediate implementation as a software prototype.
