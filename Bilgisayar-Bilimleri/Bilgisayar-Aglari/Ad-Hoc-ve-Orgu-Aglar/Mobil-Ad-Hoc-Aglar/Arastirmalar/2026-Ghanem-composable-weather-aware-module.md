# A composable weather-aware NS-3 module for compound environmental modelling in mobile ad hoc network search-and-rescue simulation

## 1. Araştırmanın özeti

> Evaluation of wireless networks under operationally relevant conditions requires consideration of environmental factors, yet many discrete-event network simulations model weather statically, in isolation or at a single layer, yielding over-optimistic results that may not transfer to operational deployments. We present a composable, component-based weather-aware module for Network Simulator 3 (NS-3) in which a single evolving environmental state is coupled across communication, mobility, line-of-sight discovery and mission-level logic. The module applies the International Telecommunication Union Radiocommunication Sector (ITU-R) attenuation models for rain, fog and atmospheric gases, together with severity-driven mobility scaling and heuristic visibility-aware line-of-sight adjustment, and integrates as an add-on to existing NS-3 configurations. We evaluate it using a paired, replicated experimental design against matched no-weather baselines, with Holm-corrected paired t -tests and paired effect sizes (Cohen’s d z ), and release the implementation as a reproducible artefact. Using a search-and-rescue mobile ad hoc network across open-space and obstacle-rich environments, we show controlled, bounded and reproducible packet-level degradation, while substantially larger operational effects emerge when the shared environmental state is coupled to mobility and line-of-sight, including reduced discovery, delayed rescue and higher duration-mediated cumulative energy. A five-level mobility-sensitivity analysis shows that mission duration increases progressively as the strength of the heuristic mobility reduction increases, with incomplete missions emerging under stronger settings. Complementary line-of-sight-retention-sensitivity experiments show progressively lower weather-feasible discovery rates under stricter fog and rain visibility assumptions. Under the full-heuristic stress-test configuration, mission duration exceeds 9000 s without completion, versus approximately 1360 s for the obstacle-rich baseline. The module provides a reusable mechanism for environmentally aware network simulation.
