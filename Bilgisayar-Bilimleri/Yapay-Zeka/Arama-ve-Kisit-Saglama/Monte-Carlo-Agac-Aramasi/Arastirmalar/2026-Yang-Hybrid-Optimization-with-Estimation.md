# Hybrid Optimization with Estimation of Distribution and Monte Carlo Tree Search for Cultural-Heritage Fragment Reconstruction

## 1. Araştırmanın özeti

> Reconstruction of cultural-heritage fragments requires selecting suitable edge correspondences in the absence of geometric and visual information. LLMFR is a new hybrid framework that integrates large language model-based evaluation with an estimation of distribution algorithm (EDA) and Monte Carlo Tree Search (MCTS). EDA samples candidate fracture edges based on an adaptive probability model, and then MCTS performs a search in the sampled candidate edges set for assemblies. A large language model evaluates the semantic consistency of the reconstructed images. Its global and local feedback updates the EDA probability model for subsequent iterations. We evaluated LLMFR qualitatively on synthetically degraded images and quantitatively on RePAIR under edge damage, pattern staining, fragment loss, and combined damage. The geometry-based GGM achieved the highest mean scores under pattern staining. LLMFR achieved higher mean adjacency F1 and correct placement rate than the geometry-based and texture-based baselines under edge damage, fragment loss, and combined damage.
