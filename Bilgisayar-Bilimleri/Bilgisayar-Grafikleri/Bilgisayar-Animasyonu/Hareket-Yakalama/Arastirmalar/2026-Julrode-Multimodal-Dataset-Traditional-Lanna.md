# A Multimodal Dataset of Traditional Lanna Dance: Motion Capture, 3D Reconstruction, and Cultural Metadata

## 1. Araştırmanın özeti

> Traditional dance is difficult to represent as research data because choreography combines whole-body movement, fine gestures, performer–object interaction, and cultural context. This Data Descriptor presents a multimodal dataset of 24 Lanna dance repertoires from Northern Thailand: 16 General Lanna dances and eight Lanna Royal dances, including 14 with props and 10 without. The collection comprises the complete set of 24 repertoires defined for this database project. Three dancers participated, with one dancer performing each repertoire and each dancer contributing eight dances; data were captured in March 2025. Each record links cultural and performance metadata with downloadable processed FBX motion data and two browser-accessible visual outputs: an interactive 3D dancer player and an MP4 rendered from the same motion-capture-driven 3D character animation. The MP4 can be viewed on the website but is not downloadable through the public interface. Capture used a Rokoko Smartsuit Pro II, Rokoko SmartGloves, and an iPhone 14 Pro Max running Rokoko Face Capture; body, hand, and facial data were recorded simultaneously using Rokoko Studio 2.6. Motion was retargeted in Blender 4.5 using the Rokoko Blender Add-on, with selective manual correction of visible artifacts. Physical props were digitized through photogrammetry using RealityCapture and optimized in Blender. The file-level audit of all 24 processed FBX files identified 736,734 frames in total, with all files at 60 fps, corresponding to 204.65 min of combined FBX animation (8.53 ± 1.36 min per file; range, 5.37–10.91 min). Case-level annotations describe movement requirements, prop interactions, and reconstruction tasks. A preliminary six-expert review documents strengths and limitations of the available digital representations. File-level screening identified endpoint and interior-frame flags; motion accuracy has not been independently validated.
