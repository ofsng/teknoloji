# A Survey of Modelling Temporal Coherence in Neural Rendering and Video Generation

## 1. Araştırmanın özeti

> Abstract With the recent surge in generative AI video models and neural rendering techniques, a key quality factor is the (spatio‐)temporal consistency of the generated or rendered sequence over its duration. Classic rendering methods, given the scene model is often readily available in full beforehand, inherently exhibit temporal and semantic consistency. This is frequently assisted for example by the appropriate antialiasing methods. Neural rendering and generative AI models, on the other hand, given rich training data, benefit from the generalisation capabilities of the advanced deep neural models to compensate for the lack of such scene attributes. The absence of a common scene model however can result in spatial and/or temporal inconsistencies in rendered sequences. This contribution aims to systematically categorise, review and compare the attributes of the surveyed methods specifically for modelling temporal coherence in neural rendering and generative AI. We group the relevant literature into six categories according to the principal mechanism through which temporal information is introduced, represented, reused or enforced. These categories cover temporal interaction within the model, temporally coherent representations, temporally coherent guidance, temporal context propagation and reuse, temporally structured diffusion noise, and lastly temporal losses, correction and smoothing. Each category is studied in detail regarding the main characteristics of the presented methods. We also provide a brief overview of the quality metrics employed by the reviewed methods for measuring temporal coherence for the related application areas. We conclude with a discussion on the future research avenues for modelling temporal coherence in this field.
