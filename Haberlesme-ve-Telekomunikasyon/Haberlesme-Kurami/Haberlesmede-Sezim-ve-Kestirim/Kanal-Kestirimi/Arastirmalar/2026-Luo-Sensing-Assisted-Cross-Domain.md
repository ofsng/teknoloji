# Sensing-Assisted Cross-Domain Channel Estimation for UAV-OFDM Systems Under Impulsive Noise

## 1. Araştırmanın özeti

> Unmanned aerial vehicles (UAVs) are widely used for power grid inspection and substation maintenance, where reliable wireless communication is essential for real-time data transmission. Orthogonal frequency-division multiplexing (OFDM) is suitable for such high-rate links, but impulsive noise in complex electromagnetic environments can severely degrade channel estimation. These short-duration, high-power disturbances may corrupt pilot symbols and spread across multiple subcarriers after OFDM demodulation, leading to large estimation errors and error floors. Therefore, robust channel estimation under impulsive noise is important for reliable UAV-to-ground communication. To address this problem, we propose a sensing-assisted cross-domain channel estimation method. A multi-symbol observation model is established in the delay–time (DT) domain, and the spreading behavior of impulsive noise in the delay–Doppler (DD) domain is analyzed. Coarse delay and Doppler bounds, assumed available as sensing-derived side information, define a DD-domain support region that suppresses out-of-support impulsive components. Residual interference is further mitigated by exploiting the column sparsity of impulsive noise in the DT domain. Based on these complementary structures, the sparse DD-domain channel and the column-sparse DT-domain impulsive noise are jointly estimated through an alternating optimization framework. Simulation results show improved channel estimation accuracy over the considered baselines under the main on-grid settings. BER gains are more limited, and performance depends on impulsive-noise and support conditions.
