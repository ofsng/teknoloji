# Phase prediction of high-entropy alloys via transfer learning with domain adaptation and oversampling

## 1. Araştırmanın özeti

> Accurate phase prediction is critical for customized high-entropy alloys (HEAs) design. Machine learning has been widely adopted to improve their phase classification accuracy, but limited samples and phase imbalance remain challenging. Herein, we developed a deep learning-based transfer learning scheme from sputtered-state to as-cast alloys. Domain adaptation method together with a multi-kernel MMD loss function is embedded to align feature distributions across different fabrication processes, and SMOTE oversampling is employed to balance the sample sizes of various phase categories between the source and target domains. For as-cast Al-Co-Cr-Cu-Fe-Ni-Mn alloy system, the developed model achieves 94 % in both precision and recall for four phase types-FCC, BCC, FCC + BCC and IM-outperforming traditional ML models by 15.6 % and 30.6 %, respectively. Validation using independent external samples also demonstrates its generalization capability. SHAP analysis reveals the influence patterns of empirical parameters and elements on HEAs phase formation. Composition-phase mapping confirm the dominant role of Al in IM formation and identifiy that Cr significantly stabilizes the FCC phase in Al-free alloys. Further experimental validation across the four phase types corroborates the model’s superior predictive performance. This work provides an effective recipe for HEAs phase prediction and lays a methodological foundation for cross-process knowledge transfer in alloy design.
