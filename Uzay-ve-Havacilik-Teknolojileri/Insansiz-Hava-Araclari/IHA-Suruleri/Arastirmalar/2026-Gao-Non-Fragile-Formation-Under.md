# Non-Fragile Formation Under Sojourn-Probability-Based Stochastic Communication for UAV Swarms

## 1. Araştırmanın özeti

> Unmanned aerial vehicle (UAV) swarms, especially large-scale swarms, often require intelligent sensing and communication due to network bandwidth and system perturbations. This paper presents a non-fragile formation control based on a sojourn-probability stochastic communication protocol for three-dimensional UAV swarms with possible control perturbations caused by a complex environment. Considering network access conflicts and bandwidth limitations, for one of the multi-agent vehicles under the stochastic communication protocol dealing with limited communication resources, the position and velocity measurements of only one random neighbor UAV are updated during the sampling interval, while the remaining neighbors’ latest measurements are held by the current UAV. Based on this communication scheduling, a discrete-time multi-agent vehicle formation system model is established, with control-command perturbations that may cause fragile formations. The perturbations are formulated as a lumped input equivalent. An adaptive observer is then designed as an estimator to estimate the lumped perturbations using asynchronously updated measurements. Then, the non-fragile formation control is designed based on the estimator and sliding-mode control techniques for reliable formations. Numerical simulations evaluate the effectiveness of the non-fragile sliding-mode formation control under the stochastic communication protocol.
