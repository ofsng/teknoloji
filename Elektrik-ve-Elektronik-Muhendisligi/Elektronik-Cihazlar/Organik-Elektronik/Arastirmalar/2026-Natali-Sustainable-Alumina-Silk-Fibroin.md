# Sustainable Alumina/Silk Fibroin Hybrid Dielectrics for Low-Temperature Fabrication of Flexible Organic Electronics

## 1. Araştırmanın özeti

> The transition toward sustainable organic electronics requires fabrication processes with reduced thermal budgets together with the integration of eco-compatible functional materials. Here, we report a sustainable hybrid dielectric platform based on low-temperature atomic layer deposition (ALD) of aluminum oxide (Al2O3) combined with silk fibroin (SF), a naturally derived protein, for flexible organic field-effect transistors (OFETs). An 80 °C ALD process was developed to deposit Al2O3 layers directly on flexible polyethylene naphthalate (PEN), chosen as a model mechanically compliant and thermally sensitive substrate. To engineer the dielectric/semiconductor interface, ultrathin SF layers were deposited on Al2O3 and compared with poly(methyl methacrylate) (PMMA) as the synthetic benchmark. Morphological, dielectric, electrical and mechanical investigations show that SF forms continuous films on low-temperature ALD Al2O3 and supports a semiconductor morphology comparable to that obtained on PMMA-buffered interfaces. OFETs incorporating Al2O3/SF hybrid dielectrics operate at gate voltages below 20 V with low leakage currents and carrier mobilities of the order of 1 cm2 V–1 s–1, and retain field-effect operation at a bending radius of 5 mm. Remarkably, this is achieved with as-cast SF, whereas PMMA requires a prolonged thermal treatment. Combining low-temperature oxide processing with natural biopolymers, this platform offers a route toward sustainable, biobased and eco-compatible organic electronics.
