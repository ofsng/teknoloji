# Design and implementation of a High-Gain Push-Pull DC-DC Converter with two internal medium-frequency transformers for photovoltaic systems

## 1. Araştırmanın özeti

> The DC-DC converter plays a key role in the integration of photovoltaic (PV) systems into DC grids and microgrids, energy storage systems, electric vehicle applications, wind power systems, and solid-state transformers. This article presents an improved high-gain push-pull DC-DC converter. Its main distinguishing feature is the use of two medium-frequency transformers (MFTs) with nanocrystalline cores, with each transformer handling half of the converter's total power. This results in the same cost and size as a conventional singletransformer design while avoiding magnetic Glux reversal, mitigating core saturation, improving thermal performance, reducing current stress through equal current sharing between the two transformers, increasing efGiciency, maintaining a simple design, and enhancing the overall performance of both the MFTs and the converter. The converter achieves a voltage conversion from 50 V to 380 V. It is validated through simulations in MATLAB/Simulink and with a 150 W experimental laboratory prototype. The converter behavior is analyzed and evaluated under different operating conditions (25%, 50%, 75%, and 100% of nominal load), achieving efGiciencies greater than 96.6% at full load words.
