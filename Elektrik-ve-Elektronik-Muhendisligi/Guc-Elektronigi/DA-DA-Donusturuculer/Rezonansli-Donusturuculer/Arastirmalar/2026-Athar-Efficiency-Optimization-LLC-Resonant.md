# Efficiency Optimization of LLC Resonant Converters via Combined Analytical Modeling and Data-Assisted Visualization

## 1. Araştırmanın özeti

> This paper introduces a comprehensive methodology to optimize efficiency in LLC resonant converters by integrating conventional analytical loss modeling with data-assisted visualization techniques. While traditional control schemes regulate output voltage via switching frequency using Pulse Frequency Modulation or duty cycle using Pulse Width Modulation, identifying the optimal operating point for maximum efficiency remains challenging, especially under wide input voltage variations common in electric vehicle chargers and industrial power supplies. Analytical loss calculations—including conduction, switching, core, and AC winding losses—are performed using established methods that account for skin effect, proximity loss, and core losses via the Steinmetz equation. However, these analytical models typically rely on ideal assumptions and may not fully capture parasitic effects, ZVS failure zones, or excessive circulating currents, which limits their accuracy in predicting peak efficiency. To overcome these challenges, a full–bridge LLC resonant converter employing 25 mΩ GaN FETs is experimentally characterized by sweeping switching frequency, duty cycle, and input voltage. The measured efficiency data are visualized in a 3D efficiency map using linear interpolation to estimate performance in unmeasured regions. This combined modeling and visualization approach enhances insight into converter behavior across multiple operating dimensions, facilitating intuitive identification of optimal points. Ultimately, this methodology improves design accuracy, accelerates prototyping, and serves as a practical tool for developing efficiency-driven control strategies.
