# Electromagnetic Compatibility Estimation between Satellite Communication Systems Located in Different Orbits

## 1. Araştırmanın özeti

> Relevance. When solving problems of national economy, artificial Earth satellites the number of which grows continuously are used. The number of satellite transmitters and receivers used to generate and transmit information signals to the Earth and receive information and commands from the Earth also grows with it. It gives rise to possible radio interference among them even though the satellites occupy different orbits and it, in its turn, makes analysis and estimation of electromagnetic compatibility actual. The purpose of this paper is to develop a procedure for elegtromagnetic compatibility analysis of radio equipment placed in the different satellises occupying different circular orbits. Methods. The extent to which interference has an effect on the reception of the useful signal is assessed by desensitization of the satellite receiver caused by the interference. The interference duration and its probability are estimated utilizing elements of the pulsed stream theory. Results. The expressions allowing to estimate the areas of satellite orbits where interference exceeds an acceptable value and possible duration of such radiation are obtained. The probability assessment of the unacceptable interference at the receiver input when the interference source communicates with the Earth employing communication sessions of a definite lengh is performed. The assessment is carryed out for deterministic periods of communications and deterministic communication sessions. The novelty. A procedure for estimation of electromagnetic compatibility between satellite communication systems is proposed. The procedure covers the satellites located in circular orbits. It refers, first of all, to low-orbital satellites which present the most part of the satellites used where the problem of electromagnetic compatibility arises most often . An assessment technique for areas of satellite orbits that can be struck by unacceptable interference is developed. The theoretical significance is determined by a design of technique that allows to detect the areas of the satellite orbits where may be unacceptable interference and the probability of its occurance. The practical significance consists in the possibility to use the developed procedure of electromagnetic compatibility analysis for a selection of orbits and operating frequences for the communication satellises.
