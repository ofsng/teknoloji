# Design of an RF MEMS Switched Metamaterial Patch Antenna for Sensing Applications

## 1. Araştırmanın özeti

> RF MEMS switches are key components in all-passive, tunable high-frequency systems. Low power consumption is also a key advantage of MEMS-based systems. This paper proposes a compact dual-band antenna system enabled by a single-pole-double-throw (SPDT) RF MEMS switch for C-Band and X-Band sensing applications. Two planar radiating elements, namely, a conventional patch antenna and a metamaterial-inspired patch embedding complementary split-ring resonators (CSRRs), are integrated within the same footprint and selectively activated through the MEMS switch. The CSRR-loaded antenna introduces additional resonant modes, enabling dual-band operation without increasing the overall system size. A λ/4 impedance transformer ensures smooth electrical matching between the SPDT and the CSRR antenna. Electromagnetic simulations using Microwave Office release 25.1 confirm good impedance matching and stable radiation patterns in both switching states, with directivity from 5.7 to 6 dB consistent with a low-profile geometry on a silicon substrate. The proposed architecture is particularly suited for reconfigurable differential sensing concepts, where access to two frequency bands can help distinguish dielectric loading from common environmental drift. In this paper, the simulated sensitivity in frequency shift per unit change in dielectric constant exceeds 100 MHz in both investigated bands.
