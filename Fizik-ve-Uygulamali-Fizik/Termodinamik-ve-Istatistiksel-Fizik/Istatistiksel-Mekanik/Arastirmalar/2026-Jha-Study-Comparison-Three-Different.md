# A Study on Comparison of Three Different Statistical Mechanics Theory

## 1. Araştırmanın özeti

> Statistical mechanics is an important tool useful in various scientific studies. It is used in studying the phase of substance, thermodynamical effects, thermodynamic quantities like pressure, volume, temperature, entropy and many more. Maxwell-Boltzmann statistics, Bose-Einstein statistics, and Fermi-Dirac statistics are theoretically compared. The main aim of this paper is to provide a fundamental idea of statistical approaches, differentiate between quantum and classical statistics and compare the three statistical approaches. Here we have used a theoretical description along with some equations that describes the concept of statistical mechanics.
