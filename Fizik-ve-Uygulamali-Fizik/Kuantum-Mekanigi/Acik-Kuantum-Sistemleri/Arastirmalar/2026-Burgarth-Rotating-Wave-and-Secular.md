# Rotating-Wave and Secular Approximations for Open Quantum Systems

## 1. Araştırmanın özeti

> We derive a nonperturbative bound on the distance between evolutions of open quantum systems described by time-dependent generators. We show how this result can be employed to provide an explicit upper bound on the error of the rotating-wave approximation in the presence of dissipation and decoherence. We apply the derived bound to the strong-coupling limit in open quantum systems and to the secular approximation used to obtain a master equation of the Gorini-Kossakowski-Lindblad-Sudarshan form from the Redfield master equation.
