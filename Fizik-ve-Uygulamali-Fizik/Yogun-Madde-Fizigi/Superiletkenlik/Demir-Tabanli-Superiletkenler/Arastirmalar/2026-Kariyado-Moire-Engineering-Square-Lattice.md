# Moiré Engineering of Square-Lattice Hubbard Models for Copper- and Iron-Based Superconductors

## 1. Araştırmanın özeti

> We propose twisted $Γ$-valley square-lattice homobilayers, exemplified by ZnF$_2$, as a tunable reference platform to emulate square lattice Hubbard-type models relevant to high-temperature superconductors. With model parameters derived from first-principles calculations, the lowest moiré band realizes a single-orbital Hubbard model underlying cuprates, while the next two realize a $p_x, p_y$ model with the minimal symmetry structure of pnictide $d_{xz}$, $d_{yz}$ Hamiltonians. Hartree-Fock analysis further predicts an antiferro-orbital ferromagnetic insulator at quarter filling.
