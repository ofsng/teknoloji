# A sequential ensemble approach to epidemic modelling: combining Hawkes and susceptible-exposed-infectious-removed models using sequential Monte Carlo squared

## 1. Araştırmanın özeti

> This paper proposes a sequential ensemble methodology for epidemic modelling that combines discrete-time Hawkes processes (DTHP) and susceptible-exposed-infectious-removed (SEIR) models. We develop a flexible model-averaging (MA) framework based on sequential Monte Carlo squared (SMC2), where model weights are dynamically updated using a sliding-window measure of each model’s recent predictive performance, derived from sequential likelihood estimates. The approach is evaluated through simulation studies involving abrupt changes in transmission dynamics and applications to influenza and COVID-19 epidemics in Ireland. Results show that combining the two models generally improves the estimation of infection trajectories and reproduction numbers compared with either model alone, particularly when individual models differ in predictive performance, with more modest gains when one model already provides a good fit to the data. The proposed MA framework delivers robust filtered estimates of the reproduction number and competitive short-term forecasts under changing epidemic conditions. These findings highlight the potential of sequential MA for real-time epidemic monitoring and decision support, while also emphasizing the challenge of detecting abrupt changes in transmission dynamics before they are reflected in observed data.
