# Protocol for CRISPR-Cas9 gene editing of murine neutrophils for in vitro and in vivo mechanistic studies

## 1. Araştırmanın özeti

> Neutrophils’ short lifespan and resistance to genetic manipulation have limited mechanistic interrogation of gene-specific functions in these cells. Here, we present a protocol for gene targeting in murine neutrophil-lineage cells using two complementary CRISPR-Cas9-based strategies. First, we describe steps for an ER-Hoxb8-based system for scalable gain- and loss-of-function studies. We then detail procedures for a Lineage− Sca-1+ cKit+ (LSK) progenitor cell-based hematopoietic stem and progenitor cell-targeting strategy for stable genetic perturbation of endogenous neutrophils.
