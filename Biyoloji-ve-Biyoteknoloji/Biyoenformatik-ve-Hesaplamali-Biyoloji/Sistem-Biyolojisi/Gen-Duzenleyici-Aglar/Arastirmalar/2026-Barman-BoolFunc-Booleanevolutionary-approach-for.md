# BoolFunc-EA: A Boolean–evolutionary approach for accurate inference of gene regulatory networks from time-series gene expression data

## 1. Araştırmanın özeti

> Network inference is the process of discovering genetic information from time-series data. The inference of gene regulatory networks from time-series data represents a significant challenge in bioinformatics. Despite the development of many Boolean network inference methods, most struggle with scalability as they impose restrictions on regulatory genes for each target gene. Other methods suffer from local optimum issues or are unable to compute the exact mutual information between a regulatory gene and its target gene. To resolve these issues, we present a novel method referred to as BoolFunc-EA, which employs Boolean functions and a genetic algorithm to infer gene regulatory networks from time-series gene expression data. BoolFunc-EA is a two-stage algorithm. First, we employ a set of limited Boolean functions to search for a regulatory gene. If a regulatory gene is not identified at this stage, a genetic algorithm is applied to determine a set of regulatory genes. In particular, this study introduces a hybrid crossover operator and a new fitness function that enhances information inheritance, improves solution evaluation, and provides a more effective solution representation within the proposed genetic algorithm framework. To assess the effectiveness of BoolFunc-EA, we conducted a wide range of experiments on both artificial and real gene expression datasets. Findings from the artificial dataset reveal that BoolFunc-EA consistently outperforms benchmark methods, including LogicGEP, LogBTF, MIBNI, LBF-MI, CLR, RelNet, Genie3-time, dynGenie3, and Jump3. The proposed method was further evaluated on the budding yeast cell cycle network and on fifty gene regulatory network datasets generated using the GNW tool. Its performance was then compared with that of nine benchmark methods. Results from real gene expression datasets also show that BoolFunc-EA consistently achieves superior F-score values, even on biological networks. Taken together, BoolFunc-EA is an efficient, scalable, and robust tool for gene regulatory network inference. The source code and reproducible experimental results are freely accessible at https://github.com/Dr-Shohag-Barman/BoolFunc-EA.
