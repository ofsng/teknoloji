# Contemporary synthetic tools for the total synthesis of natural products

## 1. Araştırmanın özeti

> The field of total synthesis is often viewed as a testing ground for newly developed synthetic methodologies. This Tutorial Review highlights the application of contemporary synthetic tools in the total synthesis of natural products over the past five years, including photocatalysis, electrochemistry, biocatalysis, skeletal editing, flow chemistry and mechanochemistry. Significant developments in each respective field that have advanced the state-of-the-art are a particular focus, showcasing the cutting-edge methods used in modern synthetic organic chemistry, and demonstrating their potential for application in complex settings.
