# Nondoped Blue and Two Color Hybrid White Organic Light Emitting Diodes Using a New Bipolar Blue Emitter

## 1. Araştırmanın özeti

> In this study, we designed and synthesized a novel bipolar blue emitter, 2-(4-(diphenylamino)phenyl)-4,6-bis(9H-carbazol-9-yl)-1,3,5-triazine (TPA-Trz-Cz2), employing a 1,3,5-triazine (Trz) core as the acceptor, with triphenylamine (TPA) and two carbazole (Cz) units as donors. A phenylene linker between TPA and Trz alleviated excessive charge transfer (CT) and enhanced the locally excited (LE) character, yielding photoluminescence quantum yields (PLQYs) of 59.9% in solution and 40.3% in neat film. TPA-Trz-Cz2 also showed excellent thermal stability, with a 5% weight-loss temperature (Td) of 517.5 °C and a glass transition temperature (Tg) of 133.2 °C. The non-doped blue device exhibited stable emission with a low turn-on voltage of 3.00 V, an external quantum efficiency (EQE) of 3.1%, an electroluminescence maximum of 471 nm, and CIE coordinates of (0.158, 0.259). Furthermore, a two-color hybrid white device stacked with a red phosphorescent layer (mCP:Ir(piq)3) achieved a maximum EQE of 7.6%, with CIE coordinates tunable to (0.349, 0.288) by controlling the layer thickness ratio. These results demonstrate that a single bipolar material can serve as both a non-doped blue emitter and a white-device component, simplifying emitting-layer design.
