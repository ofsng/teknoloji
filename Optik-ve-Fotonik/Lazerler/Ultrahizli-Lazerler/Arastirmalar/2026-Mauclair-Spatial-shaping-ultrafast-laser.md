# Spatial shaping of ultrafast laser beams for advanced material processing

## 1. Araştırmanın özeti

> Ultrafast lasers enable material processing with remarkable precision, but exploiting the increasing power of modern laser sources remains challenging. Dynamic wavefront shaping offers a powerful solution by tailoring the focal intensity distribution in space. This article introduces the main concepts and technologies behind spatial beam shaping and illustrates how shaped beams, parallel processing and structured light can improve the flexibility and efficiency of laser processing.
