# ANALYSIS OF ELECTRIC CROSSTALK IN SILICON PHOTONIC INTEGRATED CIRCUITS

## 1. Araştırmanın özeti

> Silicon Photonics offers petabyte-per-second PB/s data rates for future data links. Electrical crosstalk must be minimized for density. Discrete Mach-Zehnder Modulators are needed in silicon-based integrated photonic circuits. Building the traveling-wave electrode Coplanar strip line (CPS) and coplanar waveguide (CPW) and improving recalculation reduced crosstalk and improved system efficiency. Cutting-edge software like COMSOL Multiphysics and MATLAB was used to develop a configuration with a shared-ground coplanar waveguide (CPW) with minimal separation between the traveling wave electrode and compact dimensions, resulting in minimal interference, roughly -70dB, compared to (-21dB) for the CPS layout standard. At speeds beyond 50 Gbps, the enhanced CPW with shared-ground design ensures signal integrity and with a Bit Error Rate (BER) of 10-15 and the assured 50-ohm impedance ensures maximum interoperability with high-speed communication systems
